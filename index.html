Research

Academic Research

Published Book chapter - CRC Press/Taylor & Francis.

Book Chapter CRC Press / Taylor & Francis Group ISBN: 9781041087380

"Enhancing Medical Competency Using AR and VR Techniques"

Chapter 10 in: Surgical Robotics and Computer-Assisted Surgery: Fundamentals and Advances

Authors: Pinki Chugh, Sangita Bhowmick, Rahul Sinha Uttaranchal Institute of Technology (UIT), Uttaranchal University, Dehradun
Overview

The evolving landscape of surgical education, driven by procedural complexity and patient safety considerations, calls for modern training methods alongside traditional training. This systematic review synthesizes literature investigating the impact of Extended Reality (XR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Virtual Reality (VR) in surgical education, examining how immersive simulation tools support medical training, skill practice, and learner assessment.
